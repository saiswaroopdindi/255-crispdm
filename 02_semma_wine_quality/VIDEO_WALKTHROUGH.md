# Video walkthrough script — SEMMA / Predicting Sensory Wine Quality from Laboratory Measures

Target length: 6–8 minutes. Record screen and narration; do not state unverified metrics.

1. **0:00–0:35 | Hook.** Introduce the decision question: Test whether laboratory measures predict recorded panel scores. This experiment is not validated for triage; scores are ordinal judgments and the data do not represent every producer or vintage.
2. **0:35–1:15 | Method.** Explain SEMMA phases and why each affects the analysis.
3. **1:15–2:00 | Data.** Show provenance, schema, target, and the most important limitation.
4. **2:00–3:00 | Preparation.** Walk through split/feature choices and the leakage or scale safeguards.
5. **3:00–4:20 | Modeling.** Show baseline and candidate; explain the selection logic.
6. **4:20–5:30 | Evaluation.** Run the actual metrics, inspect the hardest slice, and state whether the proposed gate was met.
7. **5:30–6:35 | Handoff.** Show the research artifact, then state that the failed tail-recall gate prevents decision-support claims.
8. **6:35–7:00 | Close.** Summarize one lesson and the next validation step.

On-screen checklist: notebook outputs visible; no credentials or private files; dataset/source attribution; captions; disclose that results are from this dataset and are not causal claims.
