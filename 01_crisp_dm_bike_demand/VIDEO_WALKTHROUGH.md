# Video walkthrough script — CRISP-DM / Forecasting Hourly Bike Rental Demand

Target length: 6–8 minutes. Record screen and narration; do not state unverified metrics.

1. **0:00–0:35 | Hook.** Introduce the decision question: This project tests whether calendar features improve system-wide hourly demand forecasts. An aggregate total cannot identify which station needs bikes, and the evaluated candidate fails its proposed gates.
2. **0:35–1:15 | Method.** Explain CRISP-DM phases and why each affects the analysis.
3. **1:15–2:00 | Data.** Show provenance, schema, target, and the most important limitation.
4. **2:00–3:00 | Preparation.** Walk through split/feature choices and the leakage or scale safeguards.
5. **3:00–4:20 | Modeling.** Show baseline and candidate; explain the selection logic.
6. **4:20–5:30 | Evaluation.** Show the baseline beats the candidate on Q4 and explain the no-go decision.
7. **5:30–6:35 | Handoff.** Show saved artifact/sample scoring and explain required human and monitoring controls.
8. **6:35–7:00 | Close.** Summarize one lesson and the next validation step.

On-screen checklist: notebook outputs visible; no credentials or private files; dataset/source attribution; captions; disclose that results are from this dataset and are not causal claims.
